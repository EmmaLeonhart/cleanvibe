Phase 2: pan-cancer classification from mutation signatures
We classify primary tumour origin from a single SBS96 catalog across 27 TCGA projects (TCGA
MC3 30, 7,098 patients after filtering). The model is a 17.6M-parameter SAVO stack (ℎ=6, 𝐻=384,
𝑟=48, 8 encoder + 4 transition layers) with a softmax head over 27 classes; trained 8,000 steps with
AdamW + cosine-decayed LR on an 85/15 random split, single seed.
Result.
Peak held-out top-1 accuracy is 0.517 at step 2,500; the final-step checkpoint drops to 0.487
due to mild overfitting (val cross-entropy minimum is also at step 2,500). Top-5 accuracy is 0.838.
Chance baseline is 1/27 = 0.037 and majority-class baseline is 0.087 — SAVO is 5.6× better than
majority on a task whose only input is a 96-bin mutation-context histogram.
