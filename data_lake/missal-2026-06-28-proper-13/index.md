# Missal — Proper 13, Sunday June 28, 2026

**One missal.** The 12-page order of service for the **Choral Eucharist** at **Christ
Church Cathedral**, Vancouver (Anglican Church of Canada, Diocese of New Westminster).
Pulled as the project's **second** source missal, to compare against Proper 14 (July 5)
and separate the fixed frame of the rite from what changes week to week.

- **Occasion:** Proper 13 (Season after Pentecost; RCL Year A / Track 1). Local label
  "Proper 13" = US/Episcopal RCL "Proper 8" — same Sunday, different numbering.
- **Date/time:** Sunday, June 28, 2026, 10:30 a.m. — In-Person & Livestream.
- **Eucharistic prayer:** BAS Eucharistic Prayer 1 (BAS p. 193) — *different from Proper 14's
  EOW Prayer 3.*
- **Mass settings:** Gloria — New Plainsong (Hurd); Sanctus & Agnus Dei — *Mass in G minor*,
  Ralph Vaughan Williams; Lord's Prayer — McNeil Robinson.

## Principals (cover + back page)

- **Presider:** The Very Rev. Christopher A. Pappas (Dean & Rector)
- **Preacher:** The Rev. Claude St-Denis
- **Deacon:** The Rev. Kevin de la Mare
- **Organist & Director of Music:** Dr. Neil Cockburn *(the regular DoM — contrast Proper 14's
  guest organist Neil Robertson and reduced "Cathedral Quartet")*
- **Music:** The Cathedral Choir *(full choir, vs. the summer "Quartet" in Proper 14)*

## Readings

- First Reading: **Genesis 22:1-14** (the binding of Isaac / Aqedah)
- Psalm: **Psalm 13** — as a *metrical hymn paraphrase* (tune Martyrdom), not a responsorial chant
- Second Reading: **Romans 6:12-23**
- Gospel: **Matthew 10:40-42**

## Why this missal matters to the project

Same parish, same rite template, one week apart — so differences isolate the **proper**
(variable) from the **ordinary** (fixed), and the missal's own compression varies:

- **The sacring bell is PRINTED here** — "Ringing of the bell. (x3)" (page 8). Proper 14
  omitted it. Since this parish demonstrably rings the bell, its absence from the Proper 14
  missal is *compression*, not a change in practice. This one line calibrates how lossy the
  Proper 14 missal is, and justifies inferring the bell there.
- Proper 13 **prints** several things Proper 14 compressed: the full processional-hymn text,
  the full Apostles' Creed, the full Gospel acclamation dialogue, an announcements line, and
  "a period of silent reflection follows the sermon."
- It uses a **different eucharistic prayer** (BAS EP1 vs EOW EP3) and **different mass settings**
  (Vaughan Williams vs Hassler) — the "ordinary" slot is fixed in shape but variable in music.
