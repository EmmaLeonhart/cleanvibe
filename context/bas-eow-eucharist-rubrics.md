# Context — BAS / EOW Eucharist rubrics (cited)

RAG-gathered, page-cited rubrics and conventions used to justify the **inferred** events
when decompressing a missal. Scope: Choral Eucharist, Anglican Church of Canada (BAS
shape), with Eucharistic Prayer 3 from *Enriching Our Worship* (EOW) for Proper 14 and
BAS Eucharistic Prayer 1 for Proper 13.

**Category key:** **(a)** prescribed by BAS/EOW/BCP rubric · **(b)** general Anglican
custom · **(c)** optional Anglo-catholic ceremonial.

**Primary texts (read directly):**
- BAS full text — anglican.ca/wp-content/uploads/BAS.pdf (Holy Eucharist pp. 174–215)
- EOW 1 — episcopalcommonprayer.org/…/enriching_our_worship_1.pdf (EP3 pp. 62–64; Fraction Anthems p. 69)
- BCP 1979 Rite II — bcponline.org/HE/he2.html (governs ceremonial around EOW prayers)

## Shape
- BAS eucharistic action is fourfold: **taking – blessing – breaking – giving** (BAS p. 180). **(a)**
- BAS prints three section headings — "The Gathering of the Community" (p. 185), "The Proclamation of the Word" (p. 187), "The Celebration of the Eucharist" (p. 191) — + "Dismissal" (p. 214). The popular "Gathering/Word/Table/Sending" is the ecumenical label mapping onto these. **(a)/(b)**

## Postures (BAS prints very few; the rest is custom)
- **"All stand."** opens the Gathering — BAS p. 185. **(a)**
- **"All stand for the Gospel."** — BAS p. 187. **(a)**
- **"All stand"** for the Peace — BAS p. 191. **(a)**
- **"Standing, the community prays in silence"** after communion — BAS p. 213. **(a)**
- Readings/sermon: no rubric; **sitting is custom**. **(b)**
- Creed: no rubric; **standing is custom**. **(b)**
- Confession: BAS directs only **"Silence is kept"** (p. 190); **kneeling is parish custom, not rubric**. **(b)**
- Great Thanksgiving: **no stand/kneel rubric**; standing is modern norm, kneeling from the Sanctus is older/Anglo-catholic custom. **(b)/(c)**
- Communion: **"Either kneeling or standing are seen as postures appropriate for receiving communion"** — BAS p. 180. **(a)**

## Processions
- **Gospel procession** into the midst of the assembly is normal (optional) Anglican practice; may include reader, candle-bearers, thurifer, book-bearer — Episcopal Church glossary (episcopalchurch.org/glossary/gospel-procession/). **(b)**; incense form **(c)**
- **Entrance procession** (cross, ministers, choir) is universal custom for a sung eucharist; BAS assumes but doesn't choreograph it (p. 185). **(b)**

## Great Thanksgiving & manual acts
- **Sursum corda** verbatim identical in BAS p. 192 and EOW p. 62. **(a)**
- EOW EP3 shape (EOW pp. 62–64): opening dialogue → creation-focused preface → Sanctus → post-Sanctus → institution narrative → anamnesis + memorial acclamation ("Dying, you destroyed our death…") → epiclesis → doxology + Great Amen. **(a)**
- **Manual acts:** BCP Rite II rubric — *"At the words concerning the bread, the Celebrant is to hold it, or lay a hand upon it; at the words concerning the cup, to hold or place a hand upon the cup…"* (bcponline.org/HE/he2.html). **(a)** under BCP/EOW. BAS itself prints no manual-acts rubric in its prayers, so under BAS-alone the gestures are custom **(b)/(c)**. The **taking** is located at the Preparation of the Gifts (BAS p. 191). **(a)**

## Sacring bell & incense (optional, local — category c)
- Neither BAS nor EOW prescribes bells or incense.
- **Sacring/altar bell:** *"usually rung three times — once before the Words of Institution, and once at each elevation of the Host and of the Chalice"* (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altar_bell). **(c)** — **matches the Proper 13 missal's printed "Ringing of the bell. (x3)."**
- **Incense:** attested in Anglo-catholic practice generally, but **not evidenced at this cathedral's 10:30 Eucharist** (see practice doc). Not inferred for these missals.

## Fraction
- The fraction **is rubrical:** *"The celebrant breaks the consecrated bread…"* (BAS p. 211); *"The Celebrant breaks the consecrated Bread. A period of silence is kept."* (BCP). **(a)**
- **Agnus Dei / Lamb of God** provided as a fraction anthem (EOW p. 69); singing a polyphonic setting is choral custom **(b)/(c)**.

## Dismissal
- **Normally by the deacon:** *"The celebrant may bless the people. The deacon, or other leader, dismisses the people…"* (BAS p. 214). Blessing is the celebrant's and optional. **(a)/(b)**

## Music
- Choice of settings (Hurd, Hassler, Vaughan Williams…) is parish/choral custom — neither book prescribes settings. **(b)/(c)** BAS does direct the Gloria's seasonal use (used Christmas/Easter, omitted Advent/Lent) — BAS p. 185. **(a)**
