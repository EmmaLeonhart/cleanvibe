5 Results
We report text-only and multimodal scaling at three parameter scales, plus held-out OOD evaluation on
arxiv + pubmed.
5.1 60m convergence on all four modalities
Quatrix-60m (57.7M parameters) trains stably on the 4-modality mixture for 10,000 optimizer steps
with no numerical issues (Fig. 4). All four per-modality training losses decrease through the full budget;
the same stable training behaviour is observed at 120m and 180m.
