5.3 IID held-out behaviour (on-distribution)
WikiText-103 val and test are drawn from the same distribution as the training split. Performance there
measures on-distribution fit, not OOD generalization. Quatrix text-only drops 0.133 nats from train to val
(5.739→5.606); the rank-matched transformer drops 0.133 nats (5.662→5.529). The between-model
gap is 0.077 nats at every split (Fig. 7). Neither model overfits the training split more aggressively than
the other at 60m / 10,000 steps. The OOD form of the 𝑊𝑉-removal claim is addressed in §5.4.
