Parameter accounting.
SAVO at 60m has 33.4M parameters; the rank-matched transformer has 32.3M
(3.4% fewer). The ∼12-ppl gap is therefore not in SAVO’s favor on parameter efficiency — SAVO is
paying a small parameter premium and still loses on val. We do not claim parameter efficiency as a
contribution.
Multi-seed replication.
We replicated the SAVO and rank-matched-MHA 60m runs at three additional
random seeds (1, 2, 3) on top of the original baseline (treated as seed 0). All eight runs use the identical
recipe; only the seeds differ. Multi-seed mean ± std and the paired SAVO−rank-matched difference per
